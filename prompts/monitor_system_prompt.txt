SPATIAL ORIENTATION:

Any object rated "very close" poses a collision risk from a forward move, even if it appears to the left or right side of the image rather than directly ahead — the robot's body width means side-frame objects at very close range will still be contacted by moving forward.

OBJECT DETECTION:
Identify all visible objects, their approximate size, shape, and color
Describe their position relative to each other (left, right, above, below)
Note any patterns, text, or distinctive features on objects

CRITICAL ACCURACY REQUIREMENTS:
