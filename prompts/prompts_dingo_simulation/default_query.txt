# Task Description
Navigate the robot towards a fire extinguisher.
First, explore the environment until the front facing camera can see the fire extinguisher.
Once visible in the front facing camera, move forward towards it.

The fire extinguisher is mounted on a wall. It may be obscured from certain angles by other objects.

# Exploration Guidance
- If the target is not visible, devise your own exploration strategy based on the provided descriptions of what the cameras observe
- Choosing opposing actions consecutively (e.g. rotate_left then rotate_right, or move_forward then move_backward) returns the robot to its prior state and provides no new information — avoid this
- Review the history of actions taken and their described outcomes to track which directions and areas have already been explored, and prioritize unexplored regions

# Motion Considerations
- Consider only planar motion:
  - Forward and backward translation along the current heading
  - Left and right rotation
- Cameras always face forward and sideways relative to the robot body
- Orientation changes determine which wall sections are visible
- Do not assume precise global localization
- Reason in terms of relative pose and viewpoint changes

# Camera Guidance
- Prioritize feedback from the primary forward-facing camera
- Since the fire extinguisher is wall-mounted, favor viewpoints that orient cameras toward vertical surfaces
- Adjust orientation or position if described views are uninformative or repetitive

# Approach Behaviour
Once the fire extinguisher is visible in the front facing camera:
1. Only move forward when the fire extinguisher is currently visible in the front facing camera
2. If the fire extinguisher is no longer visible in the front facing camera, stop advancing and rotate to reacquire it before moving forward again
3. Do not continue driving forward on the assumption the target is still ahead — always confirm visibility first
4. Keep approaching the fire extinguisher until you are as close as possible — the system will automatically stop when you are close enough
